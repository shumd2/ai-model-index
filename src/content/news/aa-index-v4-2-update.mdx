## AA Intelligence Index v4.2: Fable 5.1 leads, new benchmarks added

Artificial Analysis published Intelligence Index v4.2 on September 4, 2026 — an interim update ahead of v5. The index now includes two new benchmarks: AA-Briefcase (agentic knowledge work) and GDP.pdf (long-document reasoning), while some older evaluations were retired.

### Key changes

- **AA-Briefcase added:** Agentic knowledge-work tasks scored as an Elo ranking
- **GDP.pdf added:** Long-document reasoning built by Surge AI
- **Fable 5.1 tops:** Leads the intelligence index following the September release
- **GLM-5.2 leads open weights:** AA 51, the new open-weights champion

### Top scores (v4.2)

| Model | AA Score | Notes |

| GPT-6 Astra | 53 | Frontier flagship |

The v4.2 index weights ten evaluations across reasoning, coding, agentic tasks, and knowledge work. Scores are independently measured by Artificial Analysis, not self-reported by providers.
