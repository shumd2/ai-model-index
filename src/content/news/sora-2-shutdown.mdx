OpenAI has shut down **Sora 2 and the Videos API**, retiring `sora-2`, `sora-2-pro` and their dated snapshots with no replacement listed. The deprecation was announced in March and took effect September 24.

For developers, the practical impact is any pipeline calling the Videos API — video generation features need a new provider or an image-sequence fallback. The move also narrows OpenAI's public model surface to text, image, audio and the realtime stack.

This site tracks the Sora line as retired in the deprecation record; no scores change since Sora models were never part of the text intelligence index.
