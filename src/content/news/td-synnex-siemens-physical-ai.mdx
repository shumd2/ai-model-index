TD SYNNEX and Siemens announced a **global partnership for IT/OT convergence and physical AI** on October 5.

The distribution partnership combines **Siemens' industrial technology** (automation, digital twin, electrification) with **TD SYNNEX's IT ecosystem** (cloud, data center, security, AI) to accelerate physical AI, automation, cybersecurity, and electrification across industries and SMBs.

Physical AI — AI that operates in the physical world through robots, industrial systems, and IoT — is the convergence layer. The partnership aims to lower the barrier for enterprises to deploy AI that spans both IT and OT environments.
