Anthropic's [September 2026 threat intelligence report](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026) describes disrupting operations where threat actors used thousands of fake accounts to extract Claude's capabilities at industrial scale — "distillation attacks" that would let bad actors recreate a frontier model without the safeguards.

This is the context for **preserved thinking**, the anti-distillation safeguard that shipped with Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5: API users can no longer edit Claude's prior context to harvest its reasoning. It applies to API accounts created on or after August 31, 2026.
