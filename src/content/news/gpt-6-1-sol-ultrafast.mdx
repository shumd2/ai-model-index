OpenAI shipped **Ultrafast mode for GPT-6.1 Sol** on October 8 across the API, Codex and ChatGPT Work — up to **8× faster** than Sol Standard, at **$12 per million input tokens and $60 per million output**.

That is roughly 1.2× Astra's rate. The speed mode carries a premium for a reason, and the premium is steep relative to the underlying $2/$10.

## Access is plan-gated, not price-gated

This is the detail that matters for subscribers. Ultrafast is available on:

- **ChatGPT Pro $500**
- Eligible **usage-based Enterprise** workspaces (admins must enable it)
- **Credit-based Edu** plans

Buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 **does not** unlock Ultrafast at launch. Within ChatGPT, Ultrafast also burns included subscription usage at 8× the standard rate — the same multiplier that applies to GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast.

## The Pro 200 cliff on October 29

Separately, eligible **Pro 200 (20×)** subscribers keep their older, higher usage allowance through **October 29, 2026**. After that date they drop to the new, lower allowance. The $200 price is unchanged, and grandfathering the allowance does **not** grant Pro 500 features or Ultrafast access.

If you are on Pro 200 and depend on the higher allowance, the decision point is late October, not now.

## Also on Bedrock

AWS made GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast available on Amazon Bedrock the same day, alongside **Claude Haiku 5.5** (Oct 7) and **reasoning summaries for OpenAI models** via the Responses API (Oct 9).
