Anthropic's CEO published ["We Must Pace the Frontier"](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier), arguing that AI progress should be deliberately paced so safety practices stay ahead of capabilities — citing competition with China, biology and cybersecurity risk, and the coming ability of models to automate AI research itself.

The essay isn't abstract: Opus 5.5 shipped a week later as the first release with external pre-release testing by Frontier Design and METR, Fable-class safeguards, and the strongest behavioral-audit scores Anthropic has measured. Whether "pacing" means slower releases or safer-faster ones is now the live question.
