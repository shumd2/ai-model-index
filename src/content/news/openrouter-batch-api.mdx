OpenRouter launched a **Batch API** with roughly **50% discounts and a 24-hour window**, live September 22 with 71 `:batch` variants — for example `gpt-6-astra:batch` at $5/$25 against the standard $10/$50.

Batch endpoints now exist at every layer of the stack: first-party (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google), cloud (Bedrock, Foundry) and routers (OpenRouter). For the registry, that means the "batch" column on model cards is increasingly a real option rather than an enterprise footnote — a 24-hour turn-around halves the cost of high-volume classification, extraction and eval jobs.

The trade-offs are the usual ones: asynchronous turnaround, and model availability that can differ from the synchronous tier. Check each provider's batch catalog for the exact variants.
