OpenAI added a **Premium seat** tier to ChatGPT Business, at **$100 per user per month billed annually or $125 billed monthly**.

| Seat type | Annual | Monthly | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $20 | $25 | Baseline per-seat allowance |
| **Premium** | **$100** | **$125** | **5× Standard, with no 5-hour limit** |

Two details distinguish Premium from simply buying more Standard seats: the **absence of a five-hour usage cap**, and the fact that Premium seats are **purchased capacity that can be reassigned between users**. If someone leaves the team, the seat moves rather than lapsing.

Standard seats are unchanged at $25 monthly / $20 annual.

## The tier ladder, for context

| Plan | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Plus | $20 |
| Business Standard seat | $25 |
| Pro 5× | $100 |
| **Business Premium seat** | **$100–125** |
| Pro 20× | $200 |
| Pro 500 | $500 |

Business Premium lands exactly where Pro 5× sits, but buys team administration, SAML SSO and the connector set instead of ChatGPT Pro's consumer features — including GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast, which is gated to Pro 500 and eligible usage-based Enterprise workspaces.

For a team that needs sustained heavy usage without a five-hour ceiling, this is the middle option between $25 Standard seats and going to Enterprise.
