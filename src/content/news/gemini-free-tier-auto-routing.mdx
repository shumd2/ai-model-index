Google's October 9 model-access change is now live. The rule that matters: **if you do not pay for an AI subscription, you can no longer choose a model.**

## What ships to whom

| Plan | Flash-Lite | Flash | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| No plan | **Auto-routed** | Auto-routed | Auto-routed |
| AI Plus | Yes | Yes | **No** |
| AI Pro | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| AI Ultra | Yes | Yes | Yes (+ Deep Think, Spark in select countries) |

For non-subscribers, the Gemini app now **routes automatically**: most prompts go to Flash-Lite, while deeper-reasoning prompts may reach Flash or Pro. If smart model selection is switched off entirely, free users get Flash-Lite for every response.

AI Plus subscribers receive a separate email about when the change applies to them.

## Effort levels still matter

Low, medium and high effort remain selectable per model — and each consumes more of the limit. Combined with automatic routing, a free user cannot guarantee which base model answers, only the effort setting.

## Current plan contents

| Plan | Models | Usage | Storage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 3.5 Flash-Lite baseline | Base | 15 GB |
| **AI Plus** $4.99 | 3.6 Flash + 3.1 Pro | 2× Free | 400 GB |
| **AI Pro** $19.99 | 3.8 Flash + 3.1 Pro | 4× Free | 5 TB |
| **AI Ultra** $99.99 / $199.99 | 3.8 Flash + 3.1 Pro + Deep Think | 5× / 20× Pro | 20 TB+ |

Limits are compute-based and refresh every 5 hours until the weekly cap. Workspace accounts are unaffected.

Worth noting for anyone choosing a tier: **AI Plus at $4.99 still has no Pro access.** It is a Flash-tier plan with extra storage and Flow credits, not a cheaper route to the Pro models.
