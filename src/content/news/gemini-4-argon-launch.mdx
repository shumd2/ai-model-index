Google DeepMind's first proprietary model above the Flash class in over seven months lands with the strongest agentic showing of any Google model to date. **Gemini 4 Argon scores 53 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at high effort** — the highest available setting — matching GPT-6 Astra (max) and edging GPT-6.1 Sol (max) by one point.

The gains concentrate exactly where Gemini has historically been weakest. Argon ranks **#1 on AutomationBench-AA at 78%**, seven points ahead of Claude Sonnet 5.5, and reaches 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 — a 53-point improvement over Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview. Its AA-Briefcase 1494 Elo is powered by a record 65% rubric pass rate, and its **15% hallucination rate on AA-Omniscience is the lowest of any model scoring 45+**, though its 50% accuracy trails Astra's 63% by 13 points: Argon is more willing to say "I don't know."

Pricing is **$4/$20 per 1M tokens at standard rates, discounted 50% to $2/$10 for at least one month**, with cached input 95% off. At promotional prices it costs $1.99 per AA task — 60% of GPT-6 Astra (max); after the promo ends, $3.98. Google has not confirmed the promotion's end date.

Availability is phased: trusted cyber defenders via the **Fairwind Program** first, then paid API customers and Google AI Ultra subscribers. The Gemini API catalog does not list Argon at the time of writing, so there is no public API model ID yet. A new **Long Decode Continuation** feature pauses long responses and resumes them across follow-up calls, enabling reasoning up to 1M output tokens without request timeouts.
