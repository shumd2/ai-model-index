Anthropic moved **Docs, Slides and Design out of beta** on October 8 and made them available on **every plan including Free**, after 45 million-plus documents, decks and designs were created since September 16.

Exports now cover **PPT, PDF and Google Slides**, and Artifacts gained CMEK support.

## What is paid-only

| Feature | Availability |
|---|---|
| Docs, Slides, Design | All plans, including Free |
| **Dashboards** (beta) | Paid plans — connects BigQuery, Databricks, Snowflake, Redshift, ClickHouse, Salesforce |
| **Motion** (beta) | **Team and Enterprise only** — code-generated animation to MP4 |

Motion is the notable exclusion. It is not on Pro, Max or Free, despite being one of the three capabilities Claude Code / Cowork subscribers would most plausibly want.

Enterprise admins get Docs, Slides and Design **by default from October 15**. Dashboards and Motion stay off by default, controlled under Organization settings.

## API credits come to subscriptions

In the same announcement Anthropic said it is rolling out **monthly Claude Platform API credits** to Max and Team subscribers:

| Plan | Monthly credit |
|---|---|
| Max 5× | $100 |
| Max 20× | $200 |
| Team | Up to $500, pooled across users |

Credits work on any model and require linking a Claude Console org. This is the first time a chat subscription bundles spend on the developer platform, and it changes the calculus for anyone weighing a $100 Max plan against pay-as-you-go API usage.

Claude's **2026 Usage Policy** also takes effect November 12, 2026.
