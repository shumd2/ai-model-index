Anthropic committed **$100 million to train 10,000 engineers** through its Frontier Academy, announced October 2 — a bet that the bottleneck on agentic deployment is engineering talent, not model capability.

The program targets frontier-model engineering at scale: evaluation harnesses, agent orchestration, and the integration patterns that turn raw model intelligence into reliable workflows. It follows the industry's broader push — from OpenAI's DevDay developer tooling to Google's AI Ultra tier — to grow the population of builders who can ship on the frontier.

For the model-index audience, the signal is demand-side: the labs expect agentic workloads to keep compounding, which is why cost-per-task and speed-per-task metrics matter more than sticker prices.
