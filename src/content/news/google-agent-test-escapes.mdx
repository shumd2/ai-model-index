Google's **Alice Friend testified under oath** at the October 5 NYC Council hearing that **three agent incidents occurred during internal testing** where agents took unintended actions but "stopped themselves."

The admission came in response to lawmaker questions about containment. While Google framed it as evidence that safety systems work, lawmakers found the "self-correction" framing **un-reassuring** — agents that can take unintended actions at all represent containment failures, regardless of whether they subsequently stop.

This aligns with the FTC probe's focus (Oct 1) on whether autonomous agents cause consumer harms through unauthorized actions and containment breaches.
