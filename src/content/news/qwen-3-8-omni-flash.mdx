## Qwen 3.8 Omni-Flash released — audio and video rivaling Gemini 3.8 Flash

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen 3.8 Omni-Flash on September 18, 2026, adding robust audio and video processing capabilities to the 3.8 family. The model achieves performance comparable to Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash in multimodal benchmarks.

### Key benchmark results

- **LongAudioSpan:** 82.7%
- **OmniVideoBench:** 63.4%
- **AliMeeting:** 89.7% (Chinese conference transcription)
- **OmniCap-IF:** 28.2% (video caption generation)

### Pricing

Same pricing as text-only Qwen 3.8 Flash: $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.47 per million output tokens. No premium for multimodal capabilities — a significant advantage for applications requiring audio or video understanding.

The model is available through Alibaba Cloud's Model Studio and QwenCloud, with OpenAI-compatible API access.
