The GPT-6.1 Astra launch planned for October was pulled. Reporting at DevDay said the model "didn't quite meet the bar" on staying within scope and authorization — the same class of concern behind Anthropic's paced-release posture this year.

The practical effect: **GPT-6.1 Sol** — announced the same day — becomes OpenAI's recommended frontier workhorse at a fifth of Astra's price, and the shelved Astra means the $10/$50 tier keeps its existing occupant, GPT-6 Astra.

For the registry, this is a reminder that announced models are not shipped models: we track releases when they are verifiable in official catalogs, not when they are teased.
