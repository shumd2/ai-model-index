Starting **October 9, 2026**, Google is updating model availability for Gemini Apps on personal accounts:

| Plan | Flash-Lite | Flash | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| No AI plan | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| AI Plus ($4.99/mo) | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| AI Pro ($19.99/mo) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| AI Ultra ($99.99/mo) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

- Free users lose Flash and Pro, keeping only **Flash-Lite** (likely 3.5 Flash-Lite)
- AI Plus subscribers lose **Pro** access (kept Flash-Lite + Flash)
- AI Pro / Ultra retain all three plus **Deep Think** (maximum parallel reasoning)
- Workspace (work/school) accounts **unaffected** per Google's help article

The changes were announced October 5 and coincide with **Gemini 4 Argon's restricted rollout** (Fairwind Program only). The consumer tier gating creates clearer separation between free and paid model access.
