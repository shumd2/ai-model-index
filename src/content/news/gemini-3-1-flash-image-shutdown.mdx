Google is retiring **`gemini-3.1-flash-image`** (Nano Banana 2, released May 28, 2026) with a shutdown date of **October 29, 2026** — just **154 days after release on 23 days' notice**.

For comparison, `gemini-2.5-flash-image` (Nano Banana 1) ran for **529 days** before its Oct 5 shutdown. The replacement is **Nano Banana 2.1**, powered by **Gemini 3.6 Flash** (per 9to5Google/GadgetsNow).

This unusually short lifecycle suggests rapid iteration in Google's native image generation line, or a consolidation around the Flash architecture. Developers using the 3.1 Flash Image API should migrate immediately.
