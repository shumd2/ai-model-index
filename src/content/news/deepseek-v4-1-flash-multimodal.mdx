## DeepSeek V4.1-Flash ships with native multimodal vision

DeepSeek officially released V4.1-Flash on September 10, 2026, adding native multimodal visual understanding to the 284-billion-parameter MoE architecture. The new pricing took effect at 04:00 UTC that day, with off-peak rates at 50% of peak pricing.

### Key benchmarks

- **Terminal-Bench 2.1:** 82.7%
- **DSBench-Hard:** 59.6%
- **Toolathlon:** 70.3%
- **DeepSWE:** 54.4%

### Architecture

The model uses a hybrid CSA/HCA attention mechanism on top of a Mixture-of-Experts backbone. 284B total parameters with only 13B activated per forward pass, maintaining the 1M-token context window and 384K max output from the V4 family.

### Pricing

Standard rates are lower than the previous V4 Flash generation. Peak/off-peak pricing continues to balance demand, with off-peak rates at exactly half the standard price. The model is available through DeepSeek's official API and third-party providers.
