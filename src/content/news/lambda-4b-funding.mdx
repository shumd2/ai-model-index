**Lambda Labs**, the Nvidia-backed AI neocloud, is raising **up to $4B at a $14.5B pre-money valuation** in a final pre-IPO round led by **Coatue Management and Blackstone** (WSJ, Oct 6).

Key metrics:
- **$50B order backlog** — up from $15B in June (3.3× growth in 4 months)
- Targeting **2027 IPO**
- Additional **$1B debt raised last week** for data-center buildouts
- WSJ print edition (Oct 7) confirms the round

Lambda's growth reflects the sustained demand for H100/H200 and upcoming B200 capacity. The neocloud model (dedicated GPU clusters with managed Kubernetes) is proving stickier than pure cloud GPU rental.
