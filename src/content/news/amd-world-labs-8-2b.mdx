AMD is acquiring **World Labs for $8.2 billion in an all-stock deal**, the merger agreement signed September 26 and announced September 28, with closing expected by the end of 2026.

World Labs — the spatial-intelligence and physical-AI lab founded by **Fei-Fei Li** — becomes the anchor of AMD's push beyond silicon into world-model software. Li joins AMD as EVP and chief scientist.

The deal is the largest AI-lab acquisition of 2026 so far and signals that hardware vendors increasingly want the model stack, not just the chips. For the model-index ecosystem, it also consolidates one of the few labs working on 3D-world generation rather than text or image output.
