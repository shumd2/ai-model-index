Google's **`gemini-2.5-flash-image`** model — released as **Nano Banana** in October 2025 — reached its **announced shutdown date on October 5, 2026**.

Developers must migrate to **Gemini 3.1 Flash Image** or **3.1 Flash Lite Image**. The deprecation was listed on the Gemini API pricing page and Firebase AI Logic docs.

This follows the pattern of Google's relatively short image-model lifecycles (see also: `gemini-3.1-flash-image` slated for Oct 29 shutdown after only 154 days).
