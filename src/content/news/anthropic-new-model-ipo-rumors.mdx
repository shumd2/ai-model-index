## Anthropic considering new model launch ahead of expected IPO

According to Reuters, Anthropic is weighing a new AI model release to counter OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra momentum. The deliberations come as the company prepares for an expected public listing and shortly after CEO Dario Amodei publicly urged the industry to slow down.

### Background

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a 3,800-word essay on September 12 calling for a deceleration in model development and warning of swarms of AI agents outpacing human control. The piece drew support from OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and other figures in the AI community.

### Why now

GPT-6 Astra's launch on September 3 gave OpenAI a significant lead on the intelligence index. With Anthropic preparing for an IPO, a competitive model release could bolster the company's market position and investor confidence. Three sources cited by Reuters confirmed the company is evaluating the timing of a potential launch.

### What to watch

Whether Anthropic can match or exceed Astra's intelligence scores, and how a new model would be positioned relative to the existing Opus, Sonnet, Haiku, Fable, and Mythos tiers.
