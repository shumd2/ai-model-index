## TypeSafe AI launches Jev — reasoning without transformers

TypeSafe AI introduced Jev on September 15, 2026 — the first "System One Model" built on a fundamentally different approach from transformer-based LLMs. Using a novel RLCD training method, Jev solves problems directly rather than generating text token-by-token.

### Claims

- **20-200x faster** than conventional LLM approaches
- **40-400x more compute-efficient**
- **GPT-5.6 Terra-level performance**
- **Reasoning without autoregressive generation**

### Background

TypeSafe AI spent two years in stealth developing a new way to train models. Founder Diogo Almeida announced the release on September 15, stating: *"I've spent the last 2 years in stealth building a new way to train models (RLCD), and a new type of frontier AI model that we are releasing today: Jev."*

### Why it matters

Every major AI lab is chasing larger transformers with more parameters. TypeSafe took a step back and asked a different question: can we solve problems without generating text? If the RLCD approach scales, it could fundamentally change the economics of AI — making frontier reasoning available at a fraction of the cost and latency of current approaches.

Jev is currently in early access with no public benchmarks or pricing yet.
