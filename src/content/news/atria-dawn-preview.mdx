## Shanghai AI Lab releases Atria Dawn Preview — 744B agentic MoE

The Shanghai AI Laboratory released Atria Dawn Preview in September 2026 — a 744-billion-parameter agentic mixture-of-experts model post-trained on Z.ai's GLM-5.2 base. Released under MIT license on Hugging Face, it achieves world-record performance on agentic benchmarks.

### Benchmark performance

- **BrowseComp:** 92.5% (world record, beats GPT-5.6 Sol at 92.2 and Claude Opus 5 at 90.8)
- **CyberGym:** 86.5% (beats GLM-5.3 at 84.5 and Sol at 83.6)
- **DeepSearchQA:** 96.0

### Architecture

Atria Dawn is built on Z.ai's GLM-5.2 base (744B total / 40B active parameters) with post-training focused on agentic capabilities. The model was released without a blog post or published pricing, with an FP8 checkpoint added September 12, 2026. The accompanying paper is signed by 143 authors and reports results across 16 benchmarks spanning research, engineering, and digital work.

### Availability

Available on Hugging Face under MIT license. No commercial API pricing has been published. The model is in preview stage.

Source: Shanghai AI Laboratory, September 2026
