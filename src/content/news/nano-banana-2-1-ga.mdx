**Gemini Nano Banana 2.1** (`gemini-nano-banana-2.1`) reached general availability on **October 6, 2026**, replacing `gemini-3.1-flash-image` (Nano Banana 2).

## What improved

Google describes it as maintaining Flash-level speed and cost efficiency while delivering gains in:

- Visual quality and prompt adherence
- **Multi-turn character consistency** — the same character across edits
- Text rendering accuracy
- Wide and panoramic aspect ratios at **1:4, 4:1, 1:8 and 8:1**
- 1K, 2K and 4K output resolutions
- Search-grounded generation

## Pricing

| Tier | Input | Output (text/thinking) | Output (images) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $1.50 | $7.50 | $30.00 |
| Batch | $0.75 | $3.75 | $15.00 |

Per image that works out to roughly **$0.0336 at 1K**, $0.0504 at 2K and $0.113 at 4K — and **$0.0168 at 1K on batch**, which is cheaper than Nano Banana 2's own batch rate of $0.034.

## The comparison that matters

Nano Banana 2 (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) charges **$60/MTok** for image output; Nano Banana 2.1 charges **$30**. At the 1K resolution that is $0.067 per image versus $0.0336 — a straight halving, with better quality and wider aspect ratios.

## Deprecation

`gemini-3.1-flash-image` is **deprecated with no shutdown date announced**. Requests keep working, so this is a migrate-when-you-can rather than a deadline.

Note the churn rate here: Nano Banana 2 launched May 28, 2026, was slated for an October 29 shutdown, and has now been superseded by a GA successor that never needed that date. Google's image line is iterating faster than its deprecation notices.
