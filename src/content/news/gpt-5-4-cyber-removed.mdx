OpenAI removed **gpt-5.4-cyber from the API** on October 1, as announced on September 11 — the cybersecurity-tuned variant of GPT-5.4 is gone from the model catalog.

The pattern this autumn is specialized frontier variants retiring first: Sora 2 on September 24, gemini-2.5-pro scheduled for October 20, and now gpt-5.4-cyber. Domain-tuned models carry extra deployment constraints, and providers are consolidating onto general-purpose flagships with strong safety controls instead.

For security workflows, the current generation equivalents are the frontier models themselves — several of which now ship enhanced cyber safeguards (Sonnet 5.5 is the latest example).
