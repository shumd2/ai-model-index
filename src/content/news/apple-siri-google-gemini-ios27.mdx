## Apple deploys rebuilt Siri powered by Google Gemini on iOS 27

Apple rolled out the rebuilt Siri on September 14, 2026, as part of iOS 27. Available on iPhone 15 Pro and newer devices, the system is powered by Google's Gemini models — a landmark partnership that brings Gemini's capabilities to hundreds of millions of Apple devices.

### What changed

The rebuilt Siri represents a fundamental rethink of Apple's voice assistant. Rather than relying on Apple's own on-device models, the new system leverages Google's Gemini for complex reasoning and natural language understanding, while keeping privacy-sensitive processing on-device.

### Availability

- iPhone 15 Pro and newer
- iOS 27 required
- Not available in the European Union

### Implications

This partnership signals a shift in how Apple approaches AI — prioritizing model quality over vertical integration. For Google, it represents a massive distribution channel for Gemini across the Apple ecosystem.
