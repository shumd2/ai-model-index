Huawei and Qualcomm announced a **multi-year patent cross-license agreement** on October 5 covering 5G, AI, and computing — a **landmark deal reversing 25 years of royalty flow**.

Key terms:
- **Qualcomm will pay Huawei net royalties for the first time** (previously Huawei paid Qualcomm)
- Qualcomm purchases certain Huawei U.S. patents in AI, compute, networking
- Expected value **>$6.9B** over the agreement term
- Requires U.S. regulatory approval

The deal signals Huawei's patent portfolio strength in AI/compute despite U.S. sanctions, and Qualcomm's need for freedom to operate in those domains. For the model ecosystem, it may accelerate cross-licensing of AI inference and accelerator IP.
