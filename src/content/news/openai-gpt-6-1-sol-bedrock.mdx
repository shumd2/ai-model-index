AWS announced **general availability of OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol on Amazon Bedrock** on October 6.

The model (released Sep 29, $2/$10 with 95% cache discounts, AA 52 at max) is now served on Bedrock with **explicit prompt caching support** for agentic workloads — the same 95% cache-read discount ($0.10/M) applies through Bedrock's pricing.

This adds Bedrock to GPT-6.1 Sol's distribution channels: OpenAI API, ChatGPT Work/Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise/Edu, Codex, Microsoft Foundry, and now Amazon Bedrock.
