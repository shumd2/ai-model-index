## AA Decision Index v1.0 (Oct 13) — a new category

Four evaluations at 25% each: **AA-Omniscience-Decision** (500 private questions), **HLE-MultiChoice** (513 HLE multiple-choice items), **When2Call** (NVIDIA) and **WildGuardTest** (Ai2).

The composite measures knowing **when to answer, when to decline and when to escalate** — a different axis from raw capability, and one that a general-purpose leaderboard has always missed.

It also formalises a category that several labs have been shipping independently. Jev 1.13 leads the dedicated decision models at **58**, with GLiDE and Microsoft-Decision-1 at 54.

The instructive result: **Claude Haiku 5.5 (Low) scores 61 overall — three points ahead of Jev 1.13**, at 1.4 s/task versus 0.13 s, and $184 versus $42 per 1M tasks. A frontier-adjacent general model still outscores models purpose-built for the job, while costing more and running slower. The category exists, but the generalists have not yet been displaced.
