The **EU AI Act's high-risk AI system enforcement phase began October 1, 2026**.

Key implications for frontier model deployments in Europe:
- **National competent authorities** must now be operational in all member states
- **Fines up to €15M or 3% of global turnover** for non-compliance
- High-risk AI systems (including certain frontier model deployments in regulated sectors) face conformity assessment, CE marking, and post-market monitoring
- The regulatory compliance burden for model deployments in regulated EU markets increases materially

This is the enforcement side of the Act that entered force Aug 1, 2024 — the two-year transition period has ended. Frontier labs and deployers should verify their EU compliance posture.
