Google's Gemini API changelog dated **October 8** deprecates two Flash generations with automatic rerouting rather than a hard shutdown:

| Model | Status | Routes to |
|---|---|---|
| `gemini-3.7-flash` | Deprecated | `gemini-3.8-flash` |
| `gemini-3.5-flash` | Deprecated | `gemini-3.6-flash` (or `gemini-3.8-flash`) |

**No shutdown date was announced for either.** Requests keep working — they just stop being addressable by the old ID in future releases.

## The Deep Research agent

`deep-research-pro-preview-12-2025` does have a hard end: it **shuts down October 23, 2026**. Migration paths are `deep-research-preview-04-2026` or `deep-research-max-preview-04-2026`.

## Already on the calendar

- `gemini-2.5-pro`, `gemini-2.5-flash`, `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` retire **October 16** on the Gemini API and **October 20** on Vertex AI
- Alibaba Cloud Model Studio retired its legacy batch models **October 10**

## A pattern worth naming

Three providers all chose reroute-over-retire in this window: Google on Flash, Xiaomi on MiMo-V2.5, and Z.ai auto-routing GLM-5.2/5.1 requests to GLM-5.3. The deprecated ID keeps working and the bill keeps landing on a successor.

It saves migration work for developers, but it also means a model can be effectively replaced without ever appearing on a shutdown list. If you pin a specific generation for reproducibility, check that your endpoint still honours the ID.
