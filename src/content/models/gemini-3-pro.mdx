## Overview

Gemini 3 Pro is Google DeepMind's frontier reasoning model, with native image, audio and video input. It holds a consistent top-20 text ranking, and its grounding variant (1207–1210) keeps Google's Search integration competitive.

It's the model to reach for inside the Google ecosystem — Workspace, Vertex AI and Search-grounded products.
