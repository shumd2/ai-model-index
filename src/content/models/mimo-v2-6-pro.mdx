## Overview

Xiaomi's MiMo line produced the surprise of 2026: **MiMo V2.6 Pro is the #1 open-weight model on the AA Intelligence Index (46)**, ahead of GLM-5.3 (45) and Kimi K3 (44).

An Omni variant adds audio and vision input. The ecosystem is younger than Qwen's or GLM's, but the raw capability per active parameter is the best in open weights.
