## Overview

Flash-Lite is Google's speed demon: **386 tokens per second** measured output — among the fastest mainstream models anywhere — with latency to match.

Quality is mid-pack by design. This is a model for realtime UX, voice pipelines and enormous volumes, not hard reasoning.
