## Overview

Jamba 1.5 (2024) pioneered the hybrid Mamba-Transformer architecture: attention where it helps, state-space layers where they're cheaper, and 256K context that actually fit in memory.

The architecture ideas went mainstream everywhere. The model itself is legacy now.
