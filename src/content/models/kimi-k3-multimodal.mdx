## Overview

Kimi K3 Multimodal is Moonshot AI's latest release, bringing native multimodal capabilities to the K3 family. With 2.8 trillion total parameters and a 1-million-token context window, it is the largest open-weights MoE model in the K3 lineup, achieving an AA Intelligence Index score of 59.7 and ranking #2 on the AA-Briefcase leaderboard with 1548 Elo.

The model is priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, making frontier-level multimodal reasoning accessible at a competitive price point.


## Why it matters

The original K3 was text-only and already impressive. Adding multimodal input — images, documents, charts — at the same intelligence level makes this model practical for real-world enterprise workflows where data arrives in multiple formats. The 2.8T parameter MoE architecture means it can handle complex tasks while maintaining reasonable inference costs.
