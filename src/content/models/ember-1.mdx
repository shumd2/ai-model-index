## Overview

Ember-1 is Fireworks AI's reasoning model, built on Kimi K3 architecture. It distinguishes itself by producing significantly shorter reasoning traces — roughly 40% fewer tokens than the base model — while maintaining comparable quality across coding, knowledge work, and agentic benchmarks.

This makes Ember-1 particularly well-suited for latency-sensitive workloads where every reasoning step costs real money. At $3/M input and $15/M output, it sits in a sweet spot between cheap-but-dumb and expensive-but-slow.

Fireworks AI provides ultra-fast inference endpoints, and Ember-1 is their flagship reasoning model as of September 2026.


## Why it matters

Most reasoning models burn tokens on extended chain-of-thought traces. Ember-1 compresses that reasoning without sacrificing quality — which means faster responses and lower costs per task. For agentic workflows that make dozens of reasoning calls per hour, the savings compound quickly.
