## Overview

Haiku 4.5 proved a small model could behave like a big one: near-Sonnet-4 quality with dramatically lower cost and latency, plus a 200K context window.

It shines as a **sub-agent** model — orchestrators like Opus delegating high-volume steps to Haiku is a very common production pattern.
