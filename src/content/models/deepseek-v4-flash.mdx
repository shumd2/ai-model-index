## Overview

The Flash name now covers DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (the old V4 Flash and its vision experiment are retired, both redirects). It's the bargain of the whole catalog: 1M context, vision input, $0.15 input and $0.60 output at peak rates, and off-peak hours halve that.

It's also one of the most-used models on earth: DeepSeek holds 25% of all OpenRouter text requests, and Flash variants are a big chunk of that.
