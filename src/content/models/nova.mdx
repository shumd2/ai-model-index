## Overview

Amazon Nova is the everything-is-fine tier of the market: Micro at roughly $0.03 per million tokens (among the cheapest measured), Lite and Pro above it, and experimental chat builds that cycle through the arenas at ranks 108–188.

You pick Nova for Bedrock integration, IAM roles and compliance checkboxes, not for leaderboard glory.
