## Overview

Kimi K3 is arguably the best **open agentic model** in the world right now: #8 on the agent arena (6.22%), **#5 on WebDev (1674)**, top-20 text (rank 17) and #3 open model on the AA Intelligence Index (44).

Self-hosting the Max variant requires serious hardware, but hosted access is inexpensive — an unusual combination of openness, quality and reach.


## Community insights

- **From "best model ever" to underrated.** When Kimi K3 launched, the community was convinced it was the best model yet — full stop. A few months later the hype has moved on and it's now *kind of underrated*, which says more about hype cycles than about the model. A top-10 agent/WebDev open model that half the community forgot about is a genuinely good entry point.
