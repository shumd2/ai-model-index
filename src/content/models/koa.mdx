## Overview

Salesforce Koa is an agentic reasoning model built on Nvidia's Nemotron architecture, trained on 27 years of internal Salesforce CRM deployment data across 14 industries. The company claims Koa makes three times fewer errors than leading general-purpose models on CRM tasks.

Koa is expected to reach general availability in winter 2026 and will be deeply integrated into the Agentforce platform, making it the first Salesforce-native reasoning model for enterprise agentic workflows.


## Why it matters

Most AI models are trained on generic internet data. Koa is different — it's trained specifically on enterprise CRM data, which means it understands sales workflows, customer relationship patterns, and enterprise data structures natively. For Salesforce customers, this could eliminate the need to adapt CRM processes around AI limitations.


## Community insights

- **CRM-native reasoning.** Unlike general models that need prompt engineering to understand CRM contexts, Koa is built from the ground up for sales and service workflows.
- **Nvidia partnership.** Built on Nemotron infrastructure, giving it enterprise-grade inference performance.
- **Three years of data.** 27 years of internal CRM deployments provide a unique training signal no other model has access to.
