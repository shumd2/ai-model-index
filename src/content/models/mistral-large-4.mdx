## Overview

**Mistral Large 4** is Mistral AI's largest model to date, announced October 6, 2026: a **1-trillion-total-parameter / 49-billion-active MoE** that is **natively multimodal** (text and image in/out). It is currently in preview on Mistral Studio and la Plateforme; an **open-weight release is planned for end of October 2026** (prior Mistral models used Apache 2.0).

The model continues Mistral's European-stack positioning: full EU data residency via Le Chat and la Plateforme, with the Medium 3.5 and Small 4 models already released in 2026 as enterprise and cost-efficient tiers.

## What we know

- **Largest Mistral model ever** — prior flagship Large 3 was ~200B-class; this is a 5× scale jump
- **First natively multimodal Mistral flagship** — prior Large models were text-only; Medium 3.5 added image in; Large 4 adds image out
- **Open-weight timeline** — Mistral has released open weights for every prior flagship (Large 1/2/3, Medium, Small); expected Apache 2.0
- **European compliance story** — Le Chat, la Plateforme, Mistral Cloud all EU-hosted; strong for regulated industries

## What we don't know (yet)

- **Pricing** — not in announcement; expect it between Medium 3.5 (enterprise) and the open-weight tier
- **Full benchmark coverage** — AA has not yet evaluated; provisional Elo/AA Index pending
- **Reasoning effort tiers** — not specified in preview announcement
- **Max output tokens** — not stated
