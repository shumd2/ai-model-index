## Overview

MiniMax M3 is a mixture-of-experts generalist holding a mid-80s text rank, with the M2.x line (down to rank 214) loved for cheap bulk agentic throughput.

MiniMax pairs the language line with strong media generation — its H3 model competes in the video arenas.
