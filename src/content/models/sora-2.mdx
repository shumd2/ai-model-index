## Overview

Sora 2 is OpenAI's video generator, in preview through Azure. It makes realistic scenes with audio from text prompts.

Interesting wrinkle: it doesn't lead the video arenas. Gemini Omni and ByteDance's Seedance currently outscore it, which says a lot about how fast video generation moved.
