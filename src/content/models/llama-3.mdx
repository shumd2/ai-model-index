## Overview

Llama 3.x (2024) is the family that made open weights normal. The 405B was the biggest open model of its year; 3.3 70B was the workhorse everyone actually ran.

The Chinese open labs and Llama 4 have passed it on quality, but the fine-tune ecosystem around 3.x is still the deepest in existence.
