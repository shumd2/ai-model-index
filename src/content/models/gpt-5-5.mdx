## Overview

GPT-5.5 spans four flavours — **Instant** (non-reasoning), **High**, **xHigh** and a **Search**-grounded variant. The xHigh tier reaches #10 on the agent arena (5.03%), while High is a top-20 text model.

It remains the default for many ChatGPT users while 5.6 rolls out, and its document-VQA performance (1486) is among the best measured.
