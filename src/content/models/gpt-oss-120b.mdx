## Overview

GPT-OSS 120B marked OpenAI's return to open weights after GPT-2: a **Mixture-of-Experts** model with 5.1B active parameters, released under Apache 2.0 alongside a 20B sibling.

It runs on a single 80GB GPU, supports adjustable reasoning effort, and — while far from frontier-closed quality — remains one of the most practical open models for self-hosting.
