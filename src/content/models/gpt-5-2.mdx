## Overview

GPT-5.2 shipped on December 11, 2025 as the reasoning refresh of the 5.x line, with a chat-latest snapshot following in early 2026. Its chat variant ranked #27 in text at its peak.

It has since been handed the retirement papers by 5.5 and 5.6, but it remains a stable, well-understood target for chat products.
