## Overview

GPT-5.2 arrived in February 2026 with a **chat-latest** snapshot tuned for conversation. Its chat variant ranked #27 in text at launch, with the reasoning tier sitting in the top 100.

It has since been overshadowed by 5.5 and 5.6, but it remains a stable, well-understood target for chat products.
