## Overview

Llama 4 (April 2025) brought Mixture-of-Experts to Meta's open line: **Scout** (17B active, 16 experts) and **Maverick** (17B active, 128 experts). Scout's headline feature remains unmatched: a **10M-token context window** — the largest measured anywhere.

Arena-wise, Llama 4 has been passed by 2026's open-model wave (Kimi, GLM, MiMo, DeepSeek). But its tooling ecosystem and deployment footprint are still the deepest in open weights.
