## Overview

Jev is TypeSafe AI's debut model, built on a fundamentally different approach from transformer-based LLMs. Using a novel RLCD training method, Jev solves problems directly rather than generating text token-by-token — reportedly achieving GPT-5.6 Terra-level performance at 20-200x the speed and 40-400x the compute efficiency of conventional approaches.

Announced September 15, 2026, Jev represents a new category of "System One Models" — AI systems that reason without the autoregressive generation paradigm that defines every other frontier model.


## Why it matters

Every major AI lab is chasing larger transformers with more parameters. TypeSafe took a step back and asked a different question: can we solve problems without generating text? If the RLCD approach scales, it could fundamentally change the economics of AI — making frontier reasoning available at a fraction of the cost and latency of current approaches.
