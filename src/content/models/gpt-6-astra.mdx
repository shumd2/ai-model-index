## Overview

GPT-6 Astra is the 2026 flagship of OpenAI's GPT line. It comes in two reasoning-effort tiers — **max** and **xhigh** — and both sit at the very top of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (53 and 52).

Its most striking result is frontend generation: Astra (max) became the first model measured above **1800 Elo** on the WebDev arena, roughly 40 points clear of second place. In chat-style voting it ranks around #24, so it trades a little conversational preference for enormous engineering capability.


## Community insights

- **"It toasts your wallet."** Astra's community nickname is *the toaster* — it toasts your wallet. When it launched, people widely assumed max-effort pricing would drop to Luna's level once the hype cooled. It never happened.
- **The pronoun debate is real.** The community genuinely cannot agree whether Astra is a he, a she, or an it. One of the most persistent running jokes in the GPT-6 threads.
- **The Instagram experiment.** Someone taught Astra to scroll Instagram and react to reels. Yes, it works exactly as well as that sounds.
- **Astra Low vs Luna Max.** The most famous community experiment so far — a head-to-head implementor test run through Codex. Read the full write-up on [r/codex](https://www.reddit.com/r/codex/comments/1w87vbw/astra_low_vs_luna_max_implementor_experiment_part/).
- **"Codex is for rich people."** The running line about doing frontier agentic coding through Codex — which is to say: bring a wallet, because the toaster is right there.
