## Overview

**GLM-5.3 Flash** reached **general availability on October 1, 2026** (research blog Aug 26, AutoClaw integration blog Oct 7) — the first **natively multimodal** model in the GLM-5 series, with a **1M-token context window** and pricing at roughly **1/10th of GLM-5.2**.

It was tested anonymously as **"ox-alpha"** on OpenCode and OpenRouter prior to release, and is now available on the Z.ai API, ZCode, Hugging Face, and OpenRouter (`z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`).

## Specs at a glance

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Context window** | 1M tokens |
| **Modalities** | Text + image in/out |
| **Reasoning** | Adaptive (implied) |

| **API IDs** | `glm-5.3-flash`, `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` |
| **Knowledge cutoff** | Not stated |

## Benchmark profile (Artificial Analysis)

| Benchmark | Score | Rank |
|---|---|---|
| **AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2** | **42** | — |
| **Image-to-WebDev (LMArena)** | **1588 Elo** | **#10** |

The Flash tier scores **AA 42** — a strong showing for a cost-optimized model — and its **Image-to-WebDev 1588 Elo places it top-10** on the LMArena WebDev leaderboard, making it a legitimate cheap WebDev player.

Cost per task on the AA Index is **~$0.25**, driven by the very low per-token pricing.

## Value proposition

- **GA, not preview** — ready for production workloads
- **1/10th GLM-5.2 price** — exceptional cost efficiency for multimodal work
- **1M context** — matches the Max variant
- **Image output** — first GLM with native image generation
- **Broad availability** — Z.ai API, ZCode, Hugging Face, OpenRouter

## Caveats

- **Not open-weight** — the GLM-5.3 Max (AA 45) is the open variant; Flash is API-only
- **Max is stronger for complex reasoning** — AA 45 vs 42; choose Max when intelligence ceiling matters
- **FlashX tier exists** at $0.37/$1.25 for higher throughput/quality
