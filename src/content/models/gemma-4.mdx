## Overview

Gemma 4 is Google's open-weight family, and Gemma 4 31B quietly reached rank 68 in the text arena. That's top-70 quality from a model that runs on a single GPU.

Sizes run from 31B down to on-device E4B, with an MoE 26B-A4B in between. For self-hosters who want Google-adjacent quality without Gemini pricing, this is the pick.
