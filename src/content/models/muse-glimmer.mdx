## Overview

Muse Glimmer is Meta's budget tier below Muse Spark: AA intelligence 17, a 131k window, and roughly **$0.06 per intelligence task**. With a sub-second time-to-first-token it's built for the unglamorous high-volume work — routing, classification, extraction — where Muse Spark would be paying premium rates to think.
