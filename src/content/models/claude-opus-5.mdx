## Overview

The Opus line is Anthropic's premium tier, and the 4.6 → 4.7 → 4.8 → 5 progression through 2026 kept it at the top of nearly every arena. Opus 4.6 (High) holds the **highest text Elo ever recorded (1505)**; Opus 5 (High) is #3 on the agent arena at 10.25%.

Opus 5 (Max) adds a 1687 WebDev showing, making the family one of the most consistently strong across categories — text, vision, documents, agents and code.
