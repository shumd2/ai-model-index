## The speed/efficiency trade-off

Solar Mini 4 is **fast per token (208 t/s)** but **heavy per task** — it burns ~7.1 minutes of output per AA Intelligence Index task, roughly **5× the cost per task of GPT-6 Luna (max)** despite similar per-token prices. This is the classic MoE pattern: sparse activation makes per-token generation cheap, but the model "thinks" extensively.

For workloads where **per-token cost dominates** (high-volume classification, routing, extraction), the $0.10/$0.40 pricing is compelling. For **long-horizon agentic tasks**, the per-task cost adds up.
