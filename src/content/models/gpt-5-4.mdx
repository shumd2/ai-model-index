## Overview

GPT-5.4 is the efficiency tier of the GPT-5.x line, available as **High**, **Mini** and **Nano**. The full model holds a top-30 text ranking; Mini and Nano trade quality for dramatically lower cost and latency.

Nano is particularly interesting for embedded and high-volume use cases, though it struggles with hard prompts — expect mid-pack behaviour there.
