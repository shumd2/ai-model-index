## Overview

Devstral 2 is Mistral's agentic coding model, and its headline number is the strangest in the index: **$0.00 per task** on Artificial Analysis' measured providers. A 256k window, 137 t/s, and open weights make it the default answer to "what can I run my coding agent on for free?"

**Devstral Small 2** is the lighter sibling — also free, also 256k.

Don't confuse it with a chat model: AA intelligence 9 reflects its specialization. In agentic coding harnesses it punches well above that.
