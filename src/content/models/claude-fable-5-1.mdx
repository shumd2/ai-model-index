## Overview

Claude Fable 5.1, launched September 1, 2026, is the most decorated model on the board right now: **#1 on the agent arena (13.71%)**, tied for #1 on the AA Intelligence Index (53), #2 on WebDev (1758) and top-5 in text voting.

Its 5.0 sibling (Fable 5 High) actually holds the highest text Elo among all Claude entries (1506). Fable is Anthropic's flagship for coding agents and knowledge work — and, per the launch note, an early glimpse of models contributing to scientific progress.
