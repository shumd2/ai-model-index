## Overview

Qwen 3.8 Flash-Next is the open-weights variant of Qwen's 3.8 Flash family, released August 26, 2026. It delivers the same strong performance as the closed Flash tier at $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.47 per million output tokens, but with a smaller 262K context window and open weights licensing.

It ranks 9th on the WebDev arena (1635 Elo) and 34th on text benchmarks, with a GDPval-AA score of 1743 — competitive with proprietary models.


## Why it matters

Open-weights access to Qwen-level performance at Flash-tier pricing is rare. While the context window is smaller than the closed variant, the model shares the same architecture and training data. For developers who need Qwen's capabilities in self-hosted or alternative API deployments, Flash-Next is the most practical option.
