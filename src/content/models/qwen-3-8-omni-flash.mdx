## Overview

Qwen 3.8 Omni-Flash is Alibaba's latest multimodal model, released September 18, 2026. It achieves audio and video processing performance comparable to Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash, with particular strength in long-form audio understanding and video captioning.

Key benchmark results include 82.7% on LongAudioSpan, 63.4% on OmniVideoBench, 89.7% on AliMeeting (multi-person Chinese conference transcription), and 28.2% on OmniCap-IF for video caption generation — surpassing Gemini 3.8 Flash in multiple benchmarks.


## Why it matters

Most Qwen models focus on text and vision. Omni-Flash adds robust audio and video processing at the same price point as the standard Flash tier — $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.47 per million output tokens. For multilingual applications requiring audio understanding, it's the most cost-effective option available.
