## Overview

Granite 4.2 is IBM's enterprise open-weight family, and the 3B tier costs about two cents per million tokens. That's not a typo.

Hybrid Mamba architectures make these models unusually good at chewing through long documents. You don't chat with Granite; you feed it paperwork at industrial scale.
