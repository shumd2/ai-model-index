## Overview

Multiverse Computing is a Spain-based company with quantum-computing roots, and its approach to LLMs is genuinely different: **tensor-network compression**. **Quasar 438B** fits a 438B-parameter model into inference running at 142 t/s with a million-token window — AA 27 at $2.02 per task.

**HyperNova 60B** (AA 12*, 131k) is the compact sibling. If the compression thesis holds at higher tiers, this is one of the most important architecture bets on the board.
