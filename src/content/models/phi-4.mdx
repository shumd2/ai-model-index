## Overview

Phi-4 (Dec 2024, 14B) was the proof that curriculum-quality synthetic data could beat scale: near-frontier 2024 reasoning from a model that ran on a laptop.

It's a museum piece now, but an important one. The Phi data thesis shows up in half the small models shipped since.
