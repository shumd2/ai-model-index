## Overview

GPT-6.1 Sol is OpenAI's fastest-moving flagship refresh on record: it replaces GPT-6 Sol just **seven days** after that model launched, and lands **one point below GPT-6 Astra** on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (52 vs 53 at max effort) at **a quarter of Astra's cost per task** ($0.72 vs $3.26).

The pricing matches GPT-6 Sol at **$2/$10 per 1M tokens**, but the cache-read discount rises from 90% to **95% ($0.10/M)**, which is where agentic workloads actually spend. The full AA effort ladder runs low 42 → medium 48 → high 50 → xhigh 51 → max 52, so even the high tier ($0.32/task) clears GPT-6 Sol's max score of 48.

Gains are concentrated in agentic work: **+4 points on AA-Briefcase v1.1, +5 on GDPval-AA v2.1, +12 on Terminal-Bench 4.0** (52% at high), and a 12-point jump in AA-Omniscience Accuracy with hallucination falling from 60% to 54%. OpenAI positions it for complex coding, computer use and professional work; it supports US and EU data residency (fast mode is unavailable with EU residency), and reasoning effort runs low/medium/high/xhigh/max — there is no `none` or `minimal` setting.

## Arena standing

Within days of launch, GPT-6.1 Sol (max) sat **#4 on the LMArena WebDev leaderboard (1758 Elo)** and **#5 in the Agent arena (0.1123 IPS)** — behind Opus 5.5, Astra, Sonnet 5.5 and Fable 5.1 in the agent rankings. It is also #10 in the vision arena.

## Value math

| Effort | AA Index | Cost per task | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| low | 42 | $0.13 | 66 t/s |
| medium | 48 | $0.21 | 59 t/s |
| high | 50 | $0.32 | 65 t/s |
| xhigh | 51 | $0.39 | 64 t/s |
| max | 52 | $0.72 | 66 t/s |

The catch is latency: max effort takes **~4.5 minutes to first token**. For most agent fleets, high effort is the sweet spot — Astra-class territory for $0.32 a task.
