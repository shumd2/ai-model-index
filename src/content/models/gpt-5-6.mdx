## Overview

GPT-5.6 continues OpenAI's habit of shipping mid-cycle frontier refreshes, this time in three named tunings: **Sol**, **Luna** and **Terra**. Sol (xHigh) is the star — it ranks #7 on the agent arena and powers a Codex-harness WebDev entry at 1604 Elo.

The tuning split seems designed for deployment styles rather than quality tiers: Sol for agents, Terra and Luna for lighter conversational and retrieval work. All three reason by default.


## Community insights

- **Luna is the people's model.** It's the low-budget pick — and **5.6 Luna has been the most popular LLM on OpenRouter for weeks**. Speaking of which: a huge share of the community routes everything through OpenRouter instead of first-party APIs.
- **"Luna Max is enough for most tasks."** A near-consensus take. Unless you specifically need Sol's agentic depth, Luna Max handles daily work just fine.
- **Next-gen Sol rumor.** Community chatter says a "GPT-6 Sol" is coming soon. Unconfirmed — but the people repeating it have been right about OpenAI's cadence before, so file it under *plausible*.
- **Tibo appreciation post.** In the Codex community, everyone loves Tibo — when he says everyone's usage limits are reset, they just… reset.
- **"Excellent stuff, please continue."** The unofficial community ritual: whenever a model finishes outputting its results, you reply exactly that. Lots of people agree. It's the way.
