## Overview

Inkling is the debut leaderboard entry from **Thinking Machines Lab** — Mira Murati's post-OpenAI venture — entering the text arena at rank 85, with Inkling Small at 141.

There's no public API at review time. The team pedigree alone makes this one of the most-watched new entrants for the next generation.
