## Overview

Mercury 2 generates 655 tokens per second by throwing out autoregression entirely: it's a diffusion model that produces tokens in parallel, the way image generators make pictures.

Quality lands around rank 211, so this is a speed play, not a quality play. But 655 t/s means a full code file renders before you lift your finger off the key.
