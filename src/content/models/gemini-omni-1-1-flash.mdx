## Overview

The Omni line is Google's natively generative multimodal family, and Omni 1.1 Flash currently leads **both** the text-to-video arena (1515) and image-to-video (1488).

Unlike classic video models, the Omni line generates synchronized audio alongside video — a meaningful advantage for creative workflows.
