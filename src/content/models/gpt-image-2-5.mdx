## Overview

GPT-Image 2.5 is OpenAI's image generation line, shipped as **Sunburst** and **Flare** variants. Sunburst currently leads **both** the text-to-image arena (1421 Elo) and the image-editing arena (1520 Elo).

Strengths include prompt adherence and text rendering inside images — historically a weak spot for generators.
