## Overview

Atria Dawn Preview is the Shanghai AI Laboratory's latest agentic model — a 744-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model post-trained on Z.ai's GLM-5.2 base. Released under MIT license on Hugging Face in September 2026, it achieved a world-record 92.5% on BrowseComp and 96.0 on DeepSearchQA, surpassing both GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 5 across multiple agentic benchmarks.

The model was released without a blog post or published pricing, with an FP8 checkpoint added September 12, 2026. The accompanying paper is signed by 143 authors and reports results across 16 benchmarks spanning research, engineering, and digital work.


## Why it matters

Atria Dawn pushes the open-weight frontier further than any previous release. Its BrowseComp score of 92.5% beats all known proprietary and open models, and its deep integration with the GLM architecture gives it a proven foundation for agentic reasoning. For researchers and developers who want frontier agentic capability without proprietary constraints, Atria Dawn is the new reference point.
