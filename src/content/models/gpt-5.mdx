## Overview

GPT-5 launched in August 2025 as the model that unified OpenAI's reasoning and fast models behind a single **auto router**. The 5.1 refresh followed in November 2025.

With a 400K context window, $1.25/$10 pricing and the largest production footprint of any model at the time, it became the default backbone for thousands of integrations. The 5.x line (5.2 through 5.6) supersedes it for new work.
