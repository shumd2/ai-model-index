## Overview

China Mobile — the world's largest mobile carrier — entered the model race with the open-weight **JT** family: **JT-4.1 Flash 236B-A21B** (AA 27*), **JT-35B-Flash** (AA 19*), and **JT-MINI** (128k).

The flagship sits in Nemotron-Ultra territory, and the deployment story is the differentiator: telecom-scale infrastructure, China-market focus, and MoE designs tuned for carrier workloads.
