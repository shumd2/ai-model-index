## Overview

The GLM-5 family (5 → 5.1 → 5.2) is the proven workhorse ladder beneath 5.3: GLM-5.2 Max holds rank 38, with the GLM-5V lineage adding vision.

For new open-weight builds, 5.3 is the pick — but 5.2 remains widely deployed and well-understood.
