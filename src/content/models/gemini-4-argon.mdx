## Overview

Gemini 4 Argon is Google DeepMind's first proprietary model above the Flash class in over seven months, and it puts Google back in the top three labs. At **high reasoning** — the highest effort available — it scores **53 on the AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2**, matching GPT-6 Astra (max) and edging GPT-6.1 Sol (max, 52), with gains driven by lower hallucinations and stronger agentic capabilities.

That is **23 points above Google's previous non-Flash model** (Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview, 30) and 12 ahead of Gemini 3.8 Flash (high). On the agentic suites it ranks **#1 on AutomationBench-AA at 78%** — seven points ahead of Sonnet 5.5 — and reaches 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, a 53-point jump over Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview, behind only Sonnet 5.5 (64%), Opus 5.5 (60%) and Astra (59%). Its AA-Briefcase 1494 rests on a **record 65% rubric pass rate**, and its **15% hallucination rate on AA-Omniscience is the lowest of any model scoring 45+** (Astra: 51%, GPT-6.1 Sol: 54%).

## Availability and pricing

**Gemini 4 Argon went generally available on the Gemini API on October 13, 2026** as `gemini-4-argon`, ending the Fairwind Program rollout. Batch, Flex and Priority inference all apply.

Pricing is **$4/$20 per 1M tokens standard, discounted 50% to $2/$10 through November 30, 2026** — Google has since published the end date the launch post omitted. From **December 1** the rate doubles to $4/$20 and cache reads rise from $0.10 to $0.20. Batch and Flex are half the standard rate; Priority is 1.8×.

At promotional prices Argon costs **$1.99 per AA task, 60% of GPT-6 Astra (max)**; after December 1 that rises to $3.98.

Argon is **not available on the Gemini API Free tier or on the consumer Google AI Pro plan** — it reaches consumers through Google AI Ultra.

## Specs

- **Model code:** `gemini-4-argon` (stable)
- **Context:** 1,048,576 tokens, unified across input and output
- **Max output:** 65,536 by default · up to **1,048,576 with long decode**, a Gemini API feature that pauses long responses and resumes them across follow-up calls so reasoning can run past request timeouts
- **Modalities:** text, image, video, audio and PDF in; text out
- **Thinking levels:** low, medium, high — there is no xhigh or max, so AA's 53 is the ceiling
- **Token use:** ~62k output tokens per AA task (vs 27k for Astra max) — efficiency comes from price, not terseness

## Two capability gaps

**Explicit context caching is not supported.** Argon does implicit caching only. On long agentic runs that re-send the same context each turn, you lose the ability to set or extend cache TTLs — a lever most competing providers treat as standard.

**No audio or image output.** Argon takes audio and images as input but produces text only, and it is not on the Live API.

It does support function calling, structured outputs, code execution, file search, URL context, search and Maps grounding, and **computer use in preview**. Agentic video understanding works but defaults to static mode.

## Read the rankings carefully

Argon's **provisional #1 on the LMArena text arena (1525.40 Elo, Oct 8 snapshot)** rests on only 4,892 votes versus 79,000+ for the incumbents, and its confidence interval overlaps with #2 through #6 — treat it as a signal, not a coronation. Its AA-Omniscience accuracy (50%) is 13 points below Astra's, so the low hallucination rate partly reflects more "I don't know" answers. AA has not published a speed figure for Argon.
